Before any AI tool touches your classroom

Ten questions to ask any AI tool

Use it for any AI tool: one a vendor is selling you, one a colleague loves, or one you built yourself. Five groups, two questions each.

Student data

  • Does it work without student names or faces?
  • Where does the work go, how long is it kept, and is it used to train AI?

Honesty

  • Does it show you what it read or used to decide?
  • When it isn't sure, does it say so instead of guessing?

You decide

  • Can you see and change every result before a student does?
  • Does it ever grade, post or message on its own?

Law and district

  • Has your district approved it?
  • For student data in New York: a signed Ed Law 2-d agreement and a privacy policy you can read?

Worth it?

  • Does it take the repeatable work and give you time back?
  • What does it cost in money, energy, and your time to check it?
Bottom line. If it can't answer these, it doesn't touch student work.
